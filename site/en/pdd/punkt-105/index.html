Kazakhstan Road Rules, item 105: No visible surface and no signs: you are on a minor road

Text of the rule

Where it is impossible to determine whether the road has a surface (hours of darkness, mud, snow and the like) and there are no priority signs, the driver shall be guided by the principle that he is on a minor road.

As amended 12.07.2026 · Source: adilet.zan.kz
Our translation from the official Russian and Kazakh texts. No official English text exists.

In plain words

If you cannot see whether the road has a surface (dark, mud, snow) and there are no priority signs, assume you are on a minor road and give way.

Common mistakes

  • Considering your road the main road because it is wider or more often used.

Check yourself

Winter, the intersection is covered with snow: you cannot tell the paved road from the dirt road, and there are no priority signs. How do you decide who goes first?

If you cannot tell whether a road is paved (darkness, mud, snow and the like) and there are no priority signs, assume you are on the minor road (Rules, item 105).
