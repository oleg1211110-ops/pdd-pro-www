Kazakhstan Road Rules, item 98: Crossed the stop line on green: drive out

Text of the rule

A driver who has passed the stop line (sign 5.33) on a permissive traffic light signal shall proceed in the intended direction regardless of the traffic light signals at the exit from the intersection. Where, at an intersection, there are stop lines and (or) sign 5.33 in accordance with Appendix 1 to these Rules in front of the traffic lights located along the driver's route, the driver shall be guided by the signals of each traffic light.

As amended 12.07.2026 · Source: adilet.zan.kz
Our translation from the official Russian and Kazakh texts. No official English text exists.

In plain words

If you have crossed the stop line (sign 5.33) on a permissive signal, continue out of the intersection in your chosen direction, even if the traffic light at the exit is already red. But if inside the intersection there are stop lines or sign 5.33 in front of the traffic lights on your route, obey each traffic light.

Common mistakes

  • Stopping in the middle of a large intersection without stop lines because of a red signal at the exit.
