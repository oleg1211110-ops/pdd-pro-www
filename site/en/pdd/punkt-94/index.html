Kazakhstan Road Rules, item 94: Controlled and uncontrolled intersection

Text of the rule

An intersection where the order of movement is determined by the signals of a traffic light or of a traffic controller is considered controlled.

When a flashing yellow signal is shown, when traffic lights are not working or when there is no traffic controller, the intersection is considered uncontrolled, and drivers shall be guided by the rules for passing uncontrolled intersections and by the priority signs installed at the intersection.

As amended 12.07.2026 · Source: adilet.zan.kz
Our translation from the official Russian and Kazakh texts. No official English text exists.

In plain words

If a traffic light or a traffic controller sets the order of movement, the intersection is controlled. If the traffic light flashes yellow, is not working, or there is no traffic controller, the intersection is uncontrolled: we follow the priority signs and the rules for uncontrolled intersections.

Common mistakes

  • Deciding that you have priority at a flashing yellow, when in fact we look at the priority signs.
