Driving theory practice test, like the real one

  • 40 questions, each with several answers and one correct.
  • 40 minutes in total; when time runs out, the test ends by itself.
  • Pass with 32 or more correct. An unanswered question counts as wrong.
  • You choose the order: skip a question and come back to it.
  • Language: Kazakh, Russian or English, your choice.

The practice test has 5 questions for now and more are being added. The full 40-question test will appear once there are enough; until then time and pass mark follow the real ratio: one minute per question and 80% correct.

Per the exam rules (Appendix 3 to Order No. 862 of the Ministry of Internal Affairs), paras 16, 18, 20, 23.
