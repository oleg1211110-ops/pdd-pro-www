Driving theory test in Astana

Astana (city of national significance). The theory test for a driving licence is taken at a special Public Service Centre (special PSC) of the State Corporation “Government for Citizens”.

Where the test is taken

  • СпецЦОН №1 — ул. Муканова, 2
  • СпецЦОН №2 — ул. Тынышбайулы, 8, блок К

Address from public data as of 01.10.2026. Before you go, check it with the 1414 contact centre or in the PSC app.

How to book

First apply at the special PSC, then choose the date and time of the theory test in the PSC app. The procedure may change, so check with 1414.

How the test works

  • 40 questions, each with several answers and one correct.
  • 40 minutes in total; when time runs out, the test ends by itself.
  • Pass with 32 or more correct. An unanswered question counts as wrong.
  • You choose the order: skip a question and come back to it.
  • Language: Kazakh, Russian or English, your choice.

Per the exam rules (Appendix 3 to Order No. 862 of the Ministry of Internal Affairs), paras 16, 18, 20, 23.
