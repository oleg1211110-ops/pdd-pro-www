Licence suspension and theory retake in Kazakhstan

From early 2026, for a number of repeat violations the licence is withdrawn but not immediately revoked: first you must re-sit the theory exam. If you fail to pass in time, the licence is annulled by the court and you must obtain it from scratch.

As of 24.11.2026

A theory retake instead of suspension

Under the Administrative Code, five kinds of repeat (within one year) violations send the driver for a theory re-examination. The licence is withdrawn, a special order is issued, and two months are given for the retake. Pass, and the licence is returned; fail, and it is annulled by court decision, after which both theory and driving must be redone.

Five violations whose repeat triggers a retake (Administrative Code)

  • art. 594 p. 4 — a repeat entry into a blocked intersection;
  • art. 596 p. 4 — repeat driving on pavements/verges, wrong position, oncoming-traffic or overtaking violations;
  • art. 598 p. 2 — repeatedly not yielding to emergency vehicles with signals on;
  • art. 599 p. 2 — repeatedly running a red light or a controller’s stop signal;
  • art. 600 p. 2 — repeatedly failing to give way to pedestrians.

What you can do yourself

To avoid a retake, don’t repeat the same violation within a year: it is the repeat that moves the article into its “heavy” part. If a retake is already set, don’t delay: prepare the theory in advance (this site has a free exam trainer and every question explained with a link to the rule) and pass within the two months given. Check your licence and order status on eGov.kz.

How to get the licence back after a suspension

After the suspension period ends the licence is not returned automatically. Usually you must: pay all fines, if required pass a medical check and get a certificate, on some grounds re-sit the exam, and then collect the licence at the special PSC. You can apply and check the status on eGov.kz or in the PSC app. The exact list of documents and the procedure depend on the ground for suspension — check with the special PSC, the 1414 contact centre and the Administrative Code on adilet.zan.kz.

How long is given for the theory retake?
Two months from being sent for re-examination. If you don’t pass, the licence is annulled by the court.

What is the MCI in 2026?
4,325 tenge. All MCI-based fines are calculated from it.

Do I need to retake the exam when getting the licence back?
On some grounds — yes. The exact list depends on the reason; check with the special PSC and the Administrative Code on adilet.zan.kz.
