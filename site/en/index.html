Sample question

An intersection of equal roads with no priority signs. You are going straight. A car is approaching the intersection from your right. Who goes first?

At an intersection of equal roads, the driver of a non-rail vehicle gives way to vehicles approaching from the right (Rules, item 103). It does not matter whether the car on the right then goes straight, left or right.
