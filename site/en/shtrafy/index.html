Traffic fines in Kazakhstan 2026

From 1 January 2026 fines are calculated from the new MCI — 4,325 ₸ (it was 3,932 ₸ in 2025). Below are the fines for common traffic violations in monthly calculation indices (MCI) and in tenge.

As of 24.11.2026

What is the MCI

The MCI is the monthly calculation index. In Kazakhstan fines are set in MCI, and the amount in tenge changes every year with its value. For 2026 the MCI is 4,325 ₸ (the law on the republican budget for 2026–2028). So a fine of “5 MCI” in 2026 is 21,625 ₸.

What you can do yourself

You can check and pay fines yourself, without intermediaries: in the eGov.kz (Mobile) app or portal, in the Sergek / road-quality app, or via banking apps (Kaspi and others) — by licence number or IIN. Paying within a short period (usually 7 days) gives a discount on many articles. If you believe a fine is unfair, you can appeal it within the set period. The exact procedure and deadlines are on egov.kz.

The amounts and article numbers are given per Kolesa.kz and Tengrinews (November 2025 – January 2026) at an MCI of 4,325 ₸ and may differ from the current wording. This is not an official document: the exact wording, articles and amounts are in the Administrative Code on adilet.zan.kz. Check the primary source before paying or appealing.
