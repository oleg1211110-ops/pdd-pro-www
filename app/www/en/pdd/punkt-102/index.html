Kazakhstan Road Rules, item 102: The main road changes direction

Text of the rule

Where the main road changes direction at an intersection, drivers moving along the main road shall be guided among themselves by the rules for passing intersections of equal roads. Drivers moving along minor roads shall be guided by the same rules.

As amended 12.07.2026 · Source: adilet.zan.kz
Our translation from the official Russian and Kazakh texts. No official English text exists.

In plain words

If the main road changes direction at the intersection, drivers on the main road pass each other by the rules of an equal-roads intersection: give way to the right. Drivers on the minor roads do the same among themselves. Even so, those on the minor roads still give way to everyone on the main road.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking that on the main road you always go first: when the main road turns, give way to the right applies among its users.

Check yourself

You are on a main road (sign 2.1) that turns at this intersection. Another car is also on the main road and approaching from your right. How do you pass each other?

When the main road changes direction at an intersection, drivers on it follow the rules for equal-road intersections among themselves (Rules, item 102), that is, give way to those on the right.

At an intersection the main road changes direction. You and another driver are on two different minor roads, and he is approaching from your right. How do you pass each other?

Drivers on minor roads at such an intersection are also guided by the rules for passing equal-road intersections (Rules, item 102).
